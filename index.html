Teaching With Clipchamp

Clipchamp is an accessible and approachable entry point into video editing. It is simple enough for a first attempt at student film-making, but retains the core logic of more advanced video editing software. This supports the learning of transferable skills and concepts.

This resource is focused on a Year 8 Media Arts class in producing a short documentary in line with the Victorian Media Arts Curriculum. It also builds the Australian Curriculum's Digital Literacy general capability, specifically the Creating and Exchanging element.

While the focus of this guide is on Media Arts, there are plenty of other learning areas such as Humanities and English that could allow for video responses where students would develop the same Creating and Exchanging Digital Literacy skills. The adjustments and implementation decisions outlined in this resource could be applicable beyond Media Arts.
