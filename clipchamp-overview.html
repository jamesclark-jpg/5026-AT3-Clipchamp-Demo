Teaching With Clipchamp

Clipchamp Overview

Why Clipchamp?

Clipchamp was selected because it's browser-based, already available to every student and teacher in a Victorian government school through the Microsoft 365 licence, supports interface localisation into 81 languages, and offers a simple, accessible interface that still retains the core logic of professional editing software.

Hands-on testing in my AT2 evaluation showed the interface minimises extraneous cognitive load, keeping students focused on learning core video production skills including logical trimming and sequencing of clips, working with transitions and adding music, effects and titles as appropriate.

ACARA Digital Literacy Element

Clipchamp develops the Creating and Exchanging element of the Digital Literacy General Capability, specifically the Create, Communicate and Collaborate sub-element: at Level 5, students are expected to "select and control advanced features of appropriate digital tools to independently create content."

Victorian Curriculum Connection

This connects to VC2AMA8C02: "apply production processes and use media arts concepts to construct representations and produce media arts works that communicate ideas, perspectives and meaning for specific audiences." With Clipchamp, students select and control advanced features such as split, trim, transitions, music and titles to compile raw footage into a coherent sequence, communicating a message for a specific audience and purpose. This supports the development of both digital literacy and understanding of media arts concepts.
